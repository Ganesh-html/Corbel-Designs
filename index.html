Frequently Asked Questions

Interior & Turnkey Contracting FAQs

Everything you need to know about working with Mumbai's premier turnkey design studio.

Corbel Designs bridges the critical disconnect between aesthetic interior architecture and on-site civil contracting. Under Director Imran Khan, we offer complete single-point accountability—meaning the team that visualizes your 3D design is the exact team executing the civil masonry, plumbing, electrical automation, and factory-finish modular joinery. This eliminates vendor finger-pointing, guarantees 100% design fidelity, and protects you from surprise cost overruns.
Yes! Our headquarters is centrally located at the iconic Boomerang Building, Chandivali Farm Road, Near Chandivali Studio, Andheri East / Powai, Mumbai - 400072. You can schedule an in-person meeting with Director Imran Khan to review physical material swatches, sample joinery mockups, and live 3D walkthroughs.
Before a single tool arrives at your site, Corbel Designs prepares an exhaustive, line-item Bill of Quantities (BOQ). We specify the exact brands, thicknesses, and grades (such as IS-710 BWP Marine ply, Blum soft-close hinges, Polycab concealed wiring, and Asian Paints Royale). Once this BOQ is signed, the cost is locked. We do not charge extra for items already scoped in your approved drawings.
While based in Chandivali / Powai, we execute turnkey residential and commercial projects throughout Mumbai, including Hiranandani Powai, Bandra West, Khar, Juhu, Andheri East & West, BKC, Lower Parel, Worli, Prabhadevi, and South Mumbai.
